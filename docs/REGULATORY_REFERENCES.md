# Regulatory context — Computer Software Assurance

> Portfolio only. Not an official CSA procedure.

## Primary FDA source

**Computer Software Assurance for Production and Quality Management System Software**  
Guidance for Industry and FDA Staff  
https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/computer-software-assurance-production-and-quality-management-system-software

**Scope (high level):** computers / automated data processing systems used as part of **medical device production or the quality management system**. Not a substitute for product software (SaMD/SiMD) controls.

**Core idea:** risk-based confidence that software is fit for intended use; rigor scales with risk to device quality/safety and related records.

## Related FDA materials

| Document | Role |
|----------|------|
